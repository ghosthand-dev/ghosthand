ghosthand has **one plan**. Sign in with Google in `ghosthand setup` and agents can use your iPhones **free for 7 days**, with every feature and no card. After that, **Ghosthand Pro is $99 per month**. [Subscribe from your dashboard](/dashboard).

## Plans

<Aside>
<Info>
One subscription covers **every Mac and iPhone** signed in with your account. No per-phone or per-seat fees.
</Info>
</Aside>

There is **no free plan** after the trial. Prices are in USD, or the same amount in EUR.

## What Pro includes

### Every device on your account

**No phone limit.** Plug in as many iPhones as you want, on as many Macs as you want. Each Mac signs in once with Google, and the [dashboard](/dashboard) lists every signed-in Mac so you can sign one out.

### The full agent toolkit

Agents drive the iPhone through the **CLI**, the **MCP server** or the **HTTP API**: screenshots over USB, on-device OCR, taps, swipes, wheel scrolls, typing, unlock, screen recording with sound, and leases so two agents never share one phone. See the [quick start](../index.mdx).

### Remote use

**Run the agent anywhere.** Add `https://ghosthand.dev/mcp` as a remote MCP server in Claude, Cursor or opencode and sign in with the same Google account. The agent reaches the iPhones on every signed-in Mac, from any computer. No port forwarding, no TLS files.

## When the trial ends

**Running work is never cut off.** ghosthand checks the plan only when an agent takes a lease (`acquire` or `wait`). A lease that is already running finishes normally.

After the trial, a new lease fails with `subscription-required`. The error tells the agent to show you the subscribe link and stop, so a scheduled run reports the link instead of retrying. Subscribe and the next lease works at once.

```bash
ghosthand account   # who is signed in, the plan, when the trial ends, the subscribe link
```

<Aside>
<Note>
If ghosthand.dev cannot be reached, the lease is **allowed**. An outage on our side never stops your agents.
</Note>
</Aside>

## Billing

**Billing runs on Stripe.** Subscribe from the [dashboard](/dashboard). After you subscribe, the same button opens the Stripe portal, where you change the card, download invoices or cancel. A canceled plan stays active until the end of the paid month.

Your screenshots, screen text and typed text **never reach ghosthand.dev**: the only thing the Mac sends is the plan check. See the [privacy policy](./privacy.mdx).
